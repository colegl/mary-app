Calendar

The kiosk reads your Google Calendar through its private address. Do this on a computer (the setting isn't in the phone app).

  1. Open Google Calendar settings (it opens in a new tab).
  2. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, click your calendar (usually your own name).
  3. Click Integrate calendar on the left. Find Secret address in iCal format, click the copy icon at its right end (two overlapping squares). If it asks, click Copy.
  4. Come back to this tab, click in the box below, paste (Ctrl+V), and click Save.

Only events whose title starts with "(Mary)" appear on the kiosk, for example (Mary) Molly visits. New events show up within 15 minutes, or click Sync calendar now.
